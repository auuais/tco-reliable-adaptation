# TUM RGB-D cross-dataset preparation

Prepare long_office_household, structure_texture_far and nostructure_texture_near_withloop from the Freiburg 3 release. These static sequences provide office and planar-scene conditions with known poses. They share a capture system and do not constitute three independent datasets. Sequence selection precedes model inference and depth-label scoring.

The published Freiburg 3 RGB calibration is fx 535.4, fy 539.2, cx 320.1, cy 247.6. The images have already been undistorted and the registered depth uses 5000 stored units per meter. Apply no additional depth correction factor. Use the same pixel-center resize affine to 518 by 392 and the same 1.12 digital zoom as in development.

Associate RGB to depth one-to-one within 20 milliseconds using deterministic global timestamp difference ordering. Use trajectory interpolation only inside a bracket with width at most 50 milliseconds, with linear translation and quaternion spherical interpolation. Source poses are evaluated at the measured depth timestamp. Target RGB poses are evaluated at RGB exposure timestamps. Select five targets at indices nearest the 10th, 30th, 50th, 70th and 90th percentile of eligible associations, requiring four preceding and following eligible neighbors. Choose t−4 and t+4 source associations for each target and exclude any target-associated depth from inference. Input selection uses timestamps and trajectory availability. Report the observed timing differences and any sequence lacking coverage.

Use both predeclared 8-by-8 source ray grids. Apply the frozen correction, local spatial comparator and per-view median with identical ranges and supplied poses. Include unchanged depth with supplied poses and ordinary frozen VGGT. Hyperparameters come from development. Report equal-sequence means, per-sequence outcomes, accuracy and completeness, and metric depth error. Use the existing 5 percent gain and 2 percent component limits and require nonnegative gains in every sequence and both grids. Motion and depth exposure differences need explicit evaluation alignment. The target depth must be motion-compensated into the target RGB camera using the supplied trajectory before pixel correspondence scoring, with a nearest-depth z-buffer and visibility mask shared by all predictions.

Download and validate archives locally while held-out scoring runs. Execute cross-dataset inference and evaluation after reviewing the held-out outcome, respecting the existing instruction to defer later spending when the method lacks a reliable improvement. Local preparation costs zero RunPod dollars. A model failure, missing coverage or unavailable download is recorded explicitly.
