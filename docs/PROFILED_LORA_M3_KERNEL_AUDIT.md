# M3 dense-kernel audit and minimum adapter

Read-only source audit, November 18, 2026. No scientific prediction/reference NPZ or scores were opened, and no GPU work was performed. This note supplements the frozen [M3 preparation contract](PROFILED_LORA_M3_PREPARATION.md); it does not change its endpoint, alignment metric, gates or execution inventory.

**What the established kernel actually measures**

`run_repeat_calibration.evaluate` uses one fixed reference mask for prediction and reference, preserving raster correspondence. It pools all five views into a scene cloud, fits one proper Umeyama similarity from corresponding pixels, then performs rigid point-to-point Open3D ICP at a 0.1 m correspondence threshold with identity incremental initialization. No RANSAC, scale-fitting ICP, per-view alignment, trimming or confidence filtering is used. `utils.umeyama` applies a determinant correction to the SVD rotation; its scale denominator is the prediction's scene-wide centered variance.

After ICP, accuracy is the mean prediction-to-reference nearest-neighbor distance; completeness is the mean reference-to-prediction distance. Both use SciPy cKDTree with `workers=-1`. **The 0.1 m threshold limits ICP correspondences, not the final metric population or distances.** All fixed-mask points enter the final nearest-neighbor metrics, including outliers and finite behind-camera predictions. These are pooled scene point means; M3 then normalizes against the same-condition P0 and averages scenes equally. Do not replace this with M2's equal-view MAE aggregation.

Normals are estimated separately on the two aligned scene clouds with Open3D's current default `estimate_normals()` settings. The reported consistency is half the sum of the two directional nearest-neighbor mean absolute normal dot products. It is unsigned consistency of estimated point-cloud normals, not an angular error against independently measured normals. Preserve the exact metric; do not silently reorient, normalize again, clamp, crop or change neighbor search.

The existing result records Sim(3) scale/rotation/translation and the ICP matrix, but not ICP fitness, inlier RMSE, correspondence count, actual iterations or the intermediate alignment objective. Those diagnostics should be added by the isolated M3 adapter without changing the transform or correspondence policy. Label fixed-pixel correspondence RMSE before/after Sim(3), ICP inlier RMSE, and final all-point accuracy/completeness separately: they are different objectives, and a drop in one need not imply a drop in another. If the installed API does not expose iteration counts, record that they are unavailable rather than inventing a convergence claim. Zero ICP correspondences or poor fitness are diagnostic outcomes, not permission to retry a different initialization or drop points.

**Conversion and metric-depth hazards**

The established resize casts depth to float64, uses OpenCV `INTER_LINEAR` from 384×512 to 392×518, and applies `K'=T K` with x/y scaling and `(scale-1)/2` principal-point shifts. It is not nearest sampling, masked interpolation, a resize of XYZ, or interpolation followed by positivity clipping. The independent SciPy half-pixel/nearest-border interpolation is a verifier with declared tolerances, not a replacement metric path.

`point_map` takes W2C, uses `C=-R^T t` and zero-skew pinhole rays, and casts world points to float32. A faithful wrapper must validate supported canonical K and rigidity of native optimized cameras rather than discarding skew or orthogonalizing matrices. The new converter isolates these operations and checks their relationship to the inverse-K/C2W expression. Main depth and centers are already metric; apply no second g0 or per-view scale. Secondary native depth and centers both receive the same g0, without relative depth scales.

Metric axial-depth MAE uses the saved resized metric depth directly, without the evaluation-fitted Sim(3) or ICP. For registered RGB, derive reference axial depth as the third row of its own pinned W2C applied to its reference points. Do not use predicted cameras to define reference depth, or M2 supplied poses in place of the reference builder's transpose/inverse convention: approximately rigid original poses make those paths subtly different. The two reference labels use different camera conventions. The smallest honest adapter reports registered-RGB axial depth only; alternate axial depth remains unsupported until its separate camera derivation is pinned and checked. Similarity alignment is allowed only for the surface protocol, not to repair metric depth.

**Common masks, boundary bins and leakage**

Reject nonfinite artifact fields. Count nonpositive main depths on the fixed reference mask and apply the frozen candidate-disqualification policy. Keep all finite fixed-mask points in auditable surface metrics; never intersect the mask with candidate confidence, positive depth, visibility, ICP inliers or low error. Record native-grid and resized validity separately when available; the registered gate uses the specified dense mask. A control's poor predictions must remain visible too.

Call `depth_spatial_support.partition` per view on reference points only. It uses Euclidean pixel distance ≤2 to an in-raster missing-depth pixel, then ≤2 to a valid horizontal/vertical 3D jump >0.05 m, then interior; missing-neighbor takes priority. Image borders alone are not missing boundaries. Check mutually exclusive masks whose union equals the common validity mask; compute them separately for each reference and retain empty-bin statuses.

Reuse only that partition logic. Its historical `residuals/collect` path fits per-view similarities and would change M3. Boundary paired-pixel residuals must use the exact same scene-wide composed transform as the surface score: `X_aligned = R_ICP*(scale*R_sim*X+t_sim)+t_ICP`. Do not fit another transform per view/bin. Paired-pixel distances differ from nearest-surface accuracy/completeness and need distinct labels. Axial boundary errors use unaligned metric depth and the registered-RGB axial reference.

Seal predictions and evaluator identities before reference decoding. Reference-based alignment/masks/bins are evaluation operations only; none may feed adapter fitting, nuisance gauge construction, confidence weighting or stopping. Reference cameras and sensor measurements used as model priors still define an oracle-prior condition. Primary/alternate references are two conventions of the same underlying observations, not independent datasets or scanner ground truth.

**Smallest faithful implementation and tests**

Use the reference-free converter, an isolated leaf evaluator preserving `evaluate` plus its exact three upstream helpers, and a thin provenance/cache/aggregation wrapper. This avoids importing the entire legacy training script and unrelated model dependencies. Bridge any extracted evaluator against the pinned original function on synthetic clouds with the same masks and runtime; added logging must not alter fitted transforms. Expose its actual final transform and diagnostic arrays once so boundary code cannot independently realign them.

Required synthetic checks: known Sim(3) plus residual rigid offset; point order/mask correspondence; asymmetric NN direction and a >0.1 m outlier retained in final metrics; finite negative depths retained but invalid for candidacy; zero/nonfinite Umeyama variance failure without a robust fallback; no extra gauge multiplication; nonuniform half-pixel resizing; rejected skew/nonrigid cameras; reference-only boundary partition/empty bins; different primary/alternate camera conventions; stale evaluator/runtime cache rejection; and two fresh-process kernel replays. These tests do not authorize real scoring before the full M3 release gate.

Supported claims remain aligned surface accuracy/completeness, unsigned estimated-normal consistency, registered-RGB metric axial error, and explicitly named boundary diagnostics on these development scenes. This kernel alone cannot establish absolute camera accuracy, calibrated uncertainty, oriented normal accuracy, physical calibration truth, unseen-scene generalization or a state-of-the-art result. No alignment change or candidate-specific filtering is an acceptable shortcut to a stronger claim.
