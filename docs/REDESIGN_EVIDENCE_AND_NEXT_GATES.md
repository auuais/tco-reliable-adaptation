# What the current evidence permits next

### Interpreting the camera-reference effect

Brachmann et al. already showed that switching between depth-SLAM and RGB SfM reference poses changes the ranking of 7-Scenes re-localisation methods. Our measured contribution so far concerns dense TCO point maps on three development sequences, the numerical effect of repeating adaptation, and the interaction between camera priors supplied to a model and the camera convention used to score it. The paired experiment with the prior convention changed inside TCO is necessary to distinguish a measurement effect from a useful adaptation intervention. Any broader claim needs separate scenes and a dataset with a different reference construction. [ICCV reference study](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/papers/Brachmann_On_the_Limits_of_Pseudo_Ground_Truth_in_Visual_Camera_ICCV_2021_paper.pdf) · [Our paired sensitivity](../results/reference_repeat_sensitivity/RESULTS.md)

For a subsequent estimator that fuses noisy camera priors, compare against a visual-only geometry solver and a fixed prior-weight solver. SIM-Sync already estimates inter-view similarities from matched keypoints lifted with learned depth. VPGGT already studies noisy pose priors and a residual connection. MASt3R-Fusion already places visual similarity constraints into a sensor factor graph. A sharper research question is whether camera priors add measurable information after uncertainty in per-view depth scale and sensor calibration is accounted for, across reasonable scoring references. Record any gain over these simpler controls before allocating transfer funds. [SIM-Sync](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05184) · [VPGGT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05531) · [MASt3R-Fusion](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S092427162600328X)

The learned matching experiments resolve one bottleneck and expose another. Coverage is now adequate on the three development scenes, but absolute acceptance is almost empty and finer marginal prior rankings are poor. Do not build a policy around the present consistency score, select a favorable threshold, or claim that transactional rollback solves this problem. It solves state accounting, not geometric correctness.

## Ordered local work after the precision comparison

1. **Establish the engineering baseline.** Validate all 29 planned precision runs, all recorded initialization/input hashes, every update and all repeat outcomes. Preserve a failure if the batch stops; never drop a scene or seed. Keep the originally frozen v7 plan as superseded and the corrected v7b execution separate. Archive to both local drives, publish all rows and verify CI.
2. **Separate prior fit from changes to geometry.** On saved initial/final predictions, measure pose/intrinsics residual changes, depth changes after explicit scale alignment, and per-view reconstruction errors. Reference quantities are offline diagnostic labels only. Determine whether apparent improvements correct cameras, dense shape, or an alignment artifact. Where saved evidence lacks a modality, record that limitation rather than infer it from an aligned point cloud.
3. **Test simple bounded and robust baselines before selection.** Predeclare normalized rotation/translation/focal residual units; compare ordinary loss, robust residual loss and a bound on displacement from the initial geometry. Keep a frozen reference branch and report when its bias prevents useful correction. A regularizer or fixed lower rate is a baseline, not a novelty claim. Use a new study ID, matched clean and controlled-corruption cases, the local offload path and a small complete denominator before expanding.
4. **Revisit a method only with a usable measurement.** A proposed marginal-constraint experiment must capture shared component gradients once, form Adam proposals from identical state, preserve all optimizer/RNG state, and use fixed validation support. Include direct-total versus summed-component gradients as an engineering control: floating-point backward passes need not sum identically. Also clear or explicitly control autocast caches when testing restored candidate parameters. No policy should use reference depth or hidden reference poses to accept a proposal.
5. **Release later spending only on reliable evidence.** A redesigned method must beat the strongest simple baseline on the development gate across seeds and repeats, improve a useful fraction of cases and retain finite, bounded failures. Then freeze its policy and follow the original order: MASt3R transfer (USD 25), held-out/cross-dataset, independent verification. Do not repurpose those allocations for a baseline-only publication after the user's explicit instruction to wait.

## Why the validation question is mathematical, not just numerical

Cross-view consistency is a necessary compatibility check, not an accuracy certificate. A reconstruction can share a common shape bias across views and have low matched-point discrepancy. A correction that improves aligned shape can increase that discrepancy. Dividing by the candidate's own geometric scale adds another dependence on shape. Our development contrasts exhibit this ranking problem. Better numerical repeatability makes a comparison more reproducible without making its objective correct.

For an adaptation step `d`, a validation gradient gives the local approximation `V(a+d)-V(a) ≈ grad(V)^T d`. A trust bound controls the neglected higher-order term only under suitable smoothness and support assumptions. It cannot turn `V` into unknown reference reconstruction error. Any claim of reliable adaptation therefore needs empirical utility on unseen scenes and explicit coverage/failure analysis, not only a descent guarantee for `V`.

## Updated baseline and novelty checks

The [TCO paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Zhou_Learning_3D_Reconstruction_with_Priors_in_Test_Time_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf) already ablates additional camera/depth-head tuning and direct 2DGS optimization, reporting worse ETH3D outcomes for those tested variants. Merely adding head tuning or optimizing renderer parameters is not an untested new contribution.

[EPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00579) already refines foundation-model reconstructions through differentiable edge alignment without explicit feature tracks; its [official repository](https://github.com/mattiadurso/EPO) supplies a practical refinement baseline. A switch to edge residuals alone would not establish novelty. [Glob3R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09225) combines a frozen geometry backbone, learned matching and global geometric optimization. [HiP3R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31635) uses hierarchical chunk/frame pose graphs for long sequences without additional training. These target different settings but make generic camera refinement, matching or pose-graph extensions insufficient novelty claims. Re-audit the full papers before selecting a contribution or comparing reported numbers.

The present outcome is a more reliable experimental foundation and several rejected hypotheses. No strong publication result has been established yet. The next method should be selected for a measurable failure mechanism and a test that could falsify it, rather than a desired conference label.
