# Decision after repeatability and controlled calibration

**Do not release later allocations.** The complete 40-job [primary calibration study](../results/repeat_calibration_v6/RESULTS.md) has no alternative passing the frozen development gate. Keep MASt3R's USD 25, held-out/cross-dataset and independent-verification allocations unspent until a redesigned method has reliable evidence. The GPU session cost approximately USD 0.72 under its USD 3 cap and is stopped.

The important new evidence is matched initialization and RNG with divergent adapted weights, plus inadequate coverage of the proposed sparse correspondence check. A favorable single run or an always-abstaining gate would not support progress. Do not tune on Office, Pumpkin, RedKitchen or Stairs to rescue this result.

The next local engineering comparison isolates **float32 direct pose-loss arithmetic**. A synthetic audit found zero rotation-angle gradients under the released autocast path at the tested 0.1–3 degree rotations, while float32 retained the expected gradient. Returning a float32 scalar did not protect the earlier matrix multiplication from rounding. This is an implementation precision issue to test, not an established reconstruction improvement or novel contribution. Both pose rotation and translation arithmetic change; backbone, camera head, K loss, rendering, weights and optimizer remain fixed.

The next frozen local study uses 29 jobs / 1,040 updates: three fresh zero controls, 18 paired precision cases over three development sequences and three seeds, and eight additional executions completing three same-seed repeats on Heads and Fire for both modes. All use the same local inactive-module offloading, five 518-pixel views, 40 steps, LR 0.0003 and released K. Initial predictions/adapters, first pose inputs and clipped gradients are retained. Aggregate only repeat 0 for the seed comparison; report the repeats separately. Require at least 5% gain, no increase in harm, no scene regression above 1%, and gain larger than both modes' maximum observed repeat range. Even a passing engineering baseline does not automatically release later funds or establish novelty.

Only after that baseline is understood should a redesigned controller proceed. The transactional shared-gradient proposal primitive is tested, but it lacks a validated selection signal. Require fixed visual support with adequate coverage and an offline demonstration that it distinguishes harmful updates; do not substitute reference reconstruction error into online decisions. Compare normalized/robust prior losses and bounded fixed-step baselines before a complicated policy. Preserve null results and abandoned branches.

The alternative-reference CPU check remains a separately reported sensitivity analysis; it must not replace the primary gate. Raw GPU evidence is already backed up on two drives. CPU-derived evidence will receive its own archive. Storage/contingency is a reserve, not a task requiring expenditure.

Before executing the local study, the cache audit found that `pose_precision_v7_local` referenced Heads-only debugging metadata. **No full-study job was attempted.** That manifest and its sources remain preserved as superseded. The replacement `pose_precision_v7b_local` uses the previously verified five-development-sequence metadata; its 29 jobs, 1,040 updates, factors and gate are unchanged. Four small integration checks of the composed capture wrapper passed, including bitwise identical zero-step outputs and verified first-gradient capture hashes. This correction is recorded explicitly rather than rewriting a frozen plan.
