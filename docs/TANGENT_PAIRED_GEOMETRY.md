# Corresponding geometry after coupled camera-depth updates

Compare the frozen joint camera/depth ensemble, its half-weight camera-prior update, and the same camera update with tangent-plane depth on the original 15 development targets. Use the archived metric depth scale. Report mean per-target squared log axial depth error and mean unsigned angular error of camera-frame normals against registered depth at matching pixels. Form centered-difference reference normals from registered depth and intrinsics; require valid positive depth at all five stencil points and reject reference jumps of 5% or more. Use identical reference support for all arms. Disclose degenerate prediction normals and assign them 90 degrees. No alignment to ground truth.

Within each image, permute the nonzero signed log-range corrections among the same changed pixels using four deterministic seeds (101, 211, 307, 401). Keep the camera, original ray and distribution fixed. Evaluate the same corresponding metrics; random permutations are controls. Also report the signed depth-error inner product and correction energy to distinguish an incorrect update direction from excessive magnitude. These are matched-pixel diagnostics on reused scenes, not a new advancement criterion.
