# Preserve local surfaces when injecting a camera prior

Changing a predicted camera while holding its axial depth fixed moves each surface patch rigidly. Test a coupled update: intersect each updated camera ray with the old tangent plane at the same image pixel, using centered horizontal and vertical differences of the pre-update, median-depth world points. Require all four neighbor depth jumps to stay under 5% of center depth, a finite normal, absolute normal/ray cosine at least 0.4, and a positive intersection within 5% of that ray's old range. Otherwise keep the original range. The center and axes of the new camera remain exactly those of the parent. No reference geometry enters the intersections.

Apply this rule independently to the equal-weight, adaptive and full-prior arms. Adaptive plus tangent-plane depth is primary. Compare to all three camera-only parents, every strength-matched control, the no-prior ensemble and earlier methods under the original two-reference surface criterion. Log eligible fractions and actual relative depth changes. Test identity and a known plane with moved camera before measuring any results. This is a development test of compatibility between camera and depth updates.
