**Scientific questions and proposed mechanism**

The original weights W0 stay frozen, with W=W0+(alpha/r)BA. The first method fits five nuisance scales on support observations, then updates LoRA on disjoint query observations while differentiating the scale solve. A fixed correspondence graph can couple the scales across cameras. The primary question is whether the extra gradient improves final reconstructed surfaces beyond a forward-identical detached-solver control. A second question is whether correlated source-camera uncertainty or a measured update size can protect that benefit under imperfect calibration.

Use the exact equations, coordinate frames, confidence normalization, support-only gauges and fixed sensor-only ridge in the mathematical specification. This five-scale layer is a surrogate for native alignment. Treat disagreement between the surrogate and the final native surface as an outcome to investigate. Relevant comparison families include TCO, CAPA, LoRA3D, Self-Geometry, Test3R, Free Geometry and IntriAdapt. Refresh the novelty review before a publication claim, using primary papers and official code.

P3 and P4 are the eligible candidates for the initial implicit-gradient hypothesis; P0/P1/P2/P5 are controls. These are resource rules, not proof of statistical significance or novelty.

Use `registered_rgb` as primary and `released` as alternate. The per-scene surface score averages accuracy and completeness distances after normalizing each component by the same-condition P0 component; aggregate equally by scene.
