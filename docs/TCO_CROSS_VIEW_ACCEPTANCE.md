# Cross-view agreement as a TCO update check

Replay all 37 adapted V6 executions against the matching scene zero-step point map, using V6's original frames 0, 200, 400, 600 and 800. These differ from the offset-25 focal development windows. Verify point-map hashes and initial camera/depth tensor identities. Use every fourth pixel in both directions on all five views. In the zero map fix query pixels with two distinct other views closer than 0.02 median initial depth and fix those two view identities. For each adapted map fit one scene similarity to the zero map using all sampled corresponding predicted points. Measure normalized nearest-surface distance over the fixed query and source-view identities, once against the original sources and once against the adapted sources. The latter measures internal agreement. A shared similarity does not change its relative shape.

The primary acceptance rule uses internal agreement: at least 5% two-view support and a 1% decrease in mean distance compared with initialization. Report benefit and harm counts for accepted and rejected runs, repeat-zero results separately, and within-scene rank associations against the original canonical score. Record fixed-source costs as a secondary check. Report the mean outcome of selecting between the original zero and the adapted map. This is a retrospective development check of an outcome-blind rule on known benchmark results, not a test of generalization. No paid compute or transfer qualification follows from this diagnostic alone.
