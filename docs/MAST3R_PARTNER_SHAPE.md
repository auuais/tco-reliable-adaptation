# Can sparse depth recover shape lost during multi-view fusion?

Registered on November 17, 2026, against crossover commit `dae21cff4c181100fb34ca2c7c87b01a6972ca23`. The earlier four-cell oracle buys at most 0.40% registered improvement over joint adaptation on these windows. This study instead expands the shape that MASt3R can express after fusing four partner predictions. The first experiment costs USD 0 in rental compute.

## Primary screen

Use Heads/seq-01, Chess/seq-03 and Fire/seq-03, frames 25, 225, 425, 625 and 825, centered 8 by 8 source-ray grid and clean supplied poses. Independently project the earlier (target minus 4) and later (target plus 4) neighboring source depth into each target. Deduplicate each packet on its own. Reuse the published calibration and hashes. Compare earlier-to-later and later-to-earlier. In each direction start from the original checkpoint and train the D adapter for 40 updates on just the fitted side. Recompute gauges, float32 forward maps, descriptors, matches and full calibrated alignment from that side. Check 20 directed pairs and 300 fine updates. Supply only the fitting packet at prediction time. Exclude any native test pixel also present in the training packet when scoring. Dense target depth is unnecessary for the screen. Neighboring measurements can share sensor and registration biases.

## Candidate and controls

Use the native single-partner canonical depth ratios on the four ordered first-image point maps, with the same 8 by 8 anchors as the fused model. Center across partners, enforce zero change at every anchor, and project off the fused relief (ratio minus one) independently within each cell. Take the leading two principal directions over the whole target image. Scale each direction by its observed between-partner variation (singular value divided by sqrt(4)). Use two coefficients per image. The shuffled control permutes whole 8 by 8 cells with the same permutation for both directions (NumPy seed 1701 plus view index), removes the new cell's relief component, and restores the two original direction norms and orthogonality. Keep the adapted matches, cameras, anchor depths, focal ratios and scale common across arms. The shape perturbation in metric axial depth is the mode times the fitted anchor depth, focal ratio and metric scale. Zero correction must reconstruct the native depth to numeric tolerance before fitting.

For this identifiability screen fit the coefficients *conditional on the training-side alignment*. Minimize mean pseudo-Huber loss with residuals divided by 2% of the target view's median fitted measurement, plus 0.01 times the sum of squared coefficients. Bound coefficients to [-1,1]. If the two-column training design has fewer than eight samples, negligible largest singular value (below 1e-8 after residual normalization), or smallest/largest singular-value ratio below 0.001, return the zero correction for that view. Record singular values, coefficients, and fallback. Use exactly the same solver and controls on the same support. Report any nonpositive depths. A nonpositive corrected evaluation sample counts as a failure for that arm; do not mask it selectively.

Primary metric is absolute axial error in meters, mean over unique test pixels in each view, then equally over the five views and equally over scenes. Report per-view and per-scene losses, counts, improvements, and reversals. Seal every full predicted depth map in both directions before scoring any held-source outcome. Advance only if the real directions strictly beat both zero and shuffled corrections in both directions on at least two of the same three scenes, and in both equally weighted aggregate directions. This is a development screen, not a significance test. Report all failures. No tuning against the held-source observations.

Secondary diagnostic registered before opening held-source scores. For each sealed two-column design, compute the minimum possible held-source absolute error within the same coefficient box using a linear program. Fit these diagnostic coefficients directly to the held readings and report the result separately as optimistic headroom. It bounds what any coefficient-selection rule for these fixed fields could achieve on those specific sparse measurements. The dense surface and joint optimizer can have different headroom. This diagnostic has no vote in the advancement rule.

As a separate calibration diagnostic, add the median signed training-depth residual to every pixel of its view, clipped to plus or minus 5% of the view's median training range. Compare its held-source MAE with the same zero control. This one-parameter classical control asks whether coherent depth bias predicts better than fine detail. Seal all 30 scalar offsets before scoring. It cannot independently advance the proposed shape method.
