# Does view-specific prior weighting add value?

The adaptive estimator assigned small mean gains. Compare it with constant gains of 0.02, 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20, with a separate scene-mean gain for centers and rotations copied from that estimator, and with its original gains cyclically assigned to the next view. The two matched controls preserve the separate mean gains. Use exactly the original aligned prior, median depths and frozen joint cameras. Reproduce the existing adaptive map hash before creating any alternative. Fit no parameters. Report both reference scores, the improvement against the matched controls and the best constant gain, and whether the adaptive method beats scene-mean weighting on all scenes. A favorable comparison against equal weighting alone does not establish a benefit from view-dependent uncertainty.
