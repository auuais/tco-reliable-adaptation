# Learn update strength from the surface objective

The previous RGB reliability estimator fit squared axial depth error. It improved the axial metric and worsened nearest-neighbor surface error after alignment. Change the fitting objective while keeping the original targets, RGB inputs, frozen features, masks, four reliability cells and leave-one-development-scene-out folds.

For each training scene use the registered reference, the direct-focal prediction and its measured similarity and rigid ICP transform. At this fixed baseline find exact nearest reference points for prediction-to-reference distances and nearest predictions for reference-to-prediction distances. For an eligible prediction with clipped motion log update u, move the point along its original camera ray by the depth fraction a*(exp(u)-1). This gives a linear displacement in 3D. Allow a in {0,0.125,0.25,0.5,1}. For each feature cell, tabulate exact Euclidean distances to the original nearest points on both sides at each strength. Normalize the two mean distances by the scene's frozen FP32 distances as in the surface evaluator, then average the two. Minimizing these fixed-association distances upper-bounds the nearest-neighbor loss at the fixed transform. A newly fitted ICP on validation data can change this relationship.

Choose strengths on the other two training scenes only. Each training scene receives equal weight; ties choose the smallest strength. Fit both four cell-specific strengths (primary) and one pooled strength (simple control). Include fixed quarter strength under the same linear depth interpolation, direct focal and all prior local, same-input and historical comparators. Verify the zero-strength objective against archived direct-focal distances and verify the learned training upper bound is no worse than zero. Report all folds and, separately, the fixed-association objective on the excluded development scene. No excluded reference enters selection of its weights. Evaluate sealed mappings with both existing surface reference conventions. Apply the unchanged 5% development surface gate before any transfer spending. This development method uses reference labels at fit time and requires independent validation even if it passes.
