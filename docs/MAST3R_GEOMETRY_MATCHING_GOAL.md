## Why this experiment

The earlier joint camera-supervised models trailed the strongest same-input controls in all four grid/pose settings. A single adapter trained with sparse axial depth and cross-pair consistency, called D in the parent study, reduced the registered surface score by about 4% on Heads and 3% on Chess relative to frozen weights with identical final alignment. Its Fire gain ranged from −0.15% to +0.11%. Across the two grids, the corresponding local depth-shape changes were about 0.52%, 0.24% and 0.09% normalized RMS. The match-set Jaccard overlaps were similar at 0.798, 0.802 and 0.793. These are retrospective observations on three development windows. [Parent results](../results/mast3r_neural_transfer/README.md) · [Shape/matching audit](MAST3R_DEPTH_FAMILY_AUDIT.md) · [Numerical repeat](../results/mast3r_neural_repeat/README.md)

MASt3R reconstructs the axial depth of pixel `p` in an 8 × 8 cell as `d_cell × [1 + (r_p − 1) × f_base/f]`. Alignment can adjust the anchor depth and focal ratio. Moving a depth boundary within the image can require a change to the local template `r_p`. The same decoder also affects discrete pixel matches and their confidence. The two changes can help or hurt each other. The crossover measures this inside the existing alignment algorithm.

## Milestone 3 — choose at most one follow-up

Use the following rule before choosing a prototype. In each grid/pose/reference setting, define a descriptive resolution margin `t = max(1e-6, 2 × largest absolute replay-minus-archive scene score across the six endpoint comparisons)`. This margin measures observed cached alignment/replay differences. Fresh adapter-training variability is measured separately in milestone 4. Persist `t` and all comparisons before fitting a candidate.

- **Geometry branch.** Nominate G1B0 if it beats both G0B0 and G1B1 by more than `t` in each of the four registered-RGB means, and is within `t` of the better endpoint or better on the released reference in each setting.
- **Observation branch.** Nominate G0B1 if it beats G0B0 by more than `t` and is within `t` of G1B1 or better, in all four registered-RGB means. Require the same direction against G0B0 and agreement with G1B1 within `t` or better on the released reference. If both nominations qualify, take the one with the larger **minimum relative gain over G0B0 across the four registered settings**. Break an exact tie in favor of the geometry hypothesis.
- **Mixed, coupled or negligible effects.** Report the interaction, per-scene reversals, repeat resolution and match diagnostics. If neither branch qualifies, finish with the complete attribution report and a local no-go recommendation. A large interaction with G1B1 winning is evidence to study the coupled pipeline. These criteria select a follow-up for this goal and leave all measured effects in the public tables.

**If geometry qualifies**, compare one small shared shape adapter to the simple G1B0 cache hybrid. Use pretrained float32 `X11` for each directed pair, frozen observations and unchanged `X21`. For pixel `p = (x,y)`, its native anchor is `a = (8⌊x/8⌋+4, 8⌊y/8⌋+4)` with zero-based coordinates. Apply `X11_new(p) = exp(0.05 × tanh(h(F_p) − h(F_a))) × X11(p)`. Implement `h` as Linear(7,16), GELU, Linear(16,1,bias=False), with only the output layer initialized to zero. Its 144 trainable parameters are shared across the five images and their ordered pairs within each independently initialized scene/grid case. The anchor multiplier stays one. The multiplier preserves the sign of each axial depth.

Build the seven inputs from three RGB channels of the native view in [0,1], three coordinates of `(X11(p) − X11(a)) / (0.02 × median_positive_depth_of_pair)` clipped to [−5,5], and `log1p(max(C11,0))` clipped to [0,5]. Fit the parent D sparse-depth and 0.1 cross-pair consistency objective on the same 40 triplets, fixed sample positions, Huber units and consistency raster. Set the fixed per-pair median gauge from the prototype's initial FP32 predictions and record any support differences from D's mixed-precision training. Use Adam at 0.001, gradient clipping at 1 and fresh seed 42. Check zero-update output identity, unchanged anchors and observation tensors, a finite nonzero gradient, and an unchanged base-model state where loaded. Rebuild canonical geometry and apply the same KDP alignment to the resulting forward cache. This bounded architecture and its hyperparameters are design choices for a single local prototype.

**If observations qualify**, keep G0 coordinates and split B into point-confidence tensors and complete weighted correspondence records. Evaluate the two missing crosses (pretrained confidences/adapted match records and their converse) over all 12 combinations. The two existing endpoints are G0B0 and G0B1.
