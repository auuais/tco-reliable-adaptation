# Calibrating RGB-motion update strength across development scenes

Use only previously sealed frozen-camera any-source and two-sided RGB triangulations. At inference partition valid positive estimates by correction sign (shorter or longer than the original predicted target) and whether the two-sided consistency rule accepted the pixel. Missing triangulations abstain. Ground-truth target masks are used only in fitting and evaluation. Fit four scalar update strengths in [0,1] on two development scenes and score the third, rotating through all three. Minimize mean per-target relative squared log-depth error, giving each physical target equal weight. Use a pooled scalar when a training cell has zero energy. Compare against abstention, the previous fixed quarter step and one pooled step trained on the exact same two scenes. Require a 2% aggregate error reduction relative to the strongest aggregate comparator and improvement on each scene before registering a distinct local surface test. Report all held-out weights, per-target outcomes and a per-cell per-target reference-assisted upper bound. This is development cross-validation, not the separate held-out dataset allocation. No RunPod cost.
