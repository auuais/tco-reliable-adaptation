# Related work for the MASt3R compensation study

The registered experiments test whether the response of a sparse depth calibration solve improves reconstruction after the same native alignment and final calibration. The first screen distinguishes whether differentiating the solve helps. The redesign tests whether penalizing the query-space change caused by refitting the scales helps beyond matched ordinary damping. Both experiments share the frozen MASt3R backbone, allowed measurements, LoRA placement, and reconstruction endpoint within each comparison. [Original contract](CALIBRATION_AWARE_LORA_GOAL.md) · [Redesign](PROFILED_COMPENSATION_REDESIGN.md)

| Method | What it establishes | Relation to this experiment |
|---|---|---|
| TCO | Test-time geometric or rendered consistency plus camera and depth prior constraints on an image-only multiview model. | The original target for transfer. P1 is the matched direct adaptation control in this MASt3R study. The released TCO objective and backbone differ and its published metrics do not provide a same-input numerical row for this table. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03878) · [Code](https://github.com/cvlab-stonybrook/TCO) |
| CAPA | Frozen-backbone LoRA or visual prompt adaptation from sparse depth, with robust affine alignment at each iteration and parameter sharing across frames. | Closest sparse-depth comparator. Repeatedly refitting depth scales is established. The isolated question here is the exact response of a fixed-weight support fit in a disjoint query objective and the additional compensation penalty. An accuracy claim beyond this study requires a matched-input benchmark. [Paper, §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14751v1#S3.SS3) · [Project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dvl/projects/capa/) |
| LoRA3D | Confidence calibration and robust global alignment provide pseudo-labels for LoRA from RGB views alone. | A relevant test-time backbone adaptation baseline. P0 and P1 control refinement and adaptation for the allowed external measurements, respectively. Neither reproduces LoRA3D’s pseudo-labeling or confidence optimization. [ICLR paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/6db7c49b14da8006892fda7350d76b6a-Paper-Conference.pdf) |
| Test3R | Shared-reference cross-pair geometry supervises visual prompt adaptation from RGB. The paper includes MASt3R experiments. | A same-backbone image-only baseline, with a different objective and information budget. Published image-only improvements cannot establish the incremental contribution of a depth-informed adapter without matched-input controls. [NeurIPS paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/911dd89c81efc624c4e1c39381179505-Paper-Conference.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/nopQAQ/Test3R) |
| Self-Geometry | LoRA with correspondence-based multi-view and epipolar consistency and gradient disentanglement. | Establishes a precedent for geometry-specific control of test-time gradients. R1 regularizes the fitted scale response in query depth and R2 detaches that solve at the same output. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10708) |
| Free Geometry | Uses longer and shorter sets of views from a reconstruction as self-supervision for LoRA, including on VGGT. | A different source of adaptation supervision. Neither its published improvements nor a VGGT-only result would provide measured evidence for this MASt3R calibration mechanism. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14048) |
| IntriAdapt | Optimizes LoRA using alignment of predicted rays with known intrinsics, and reports adaptation across sequences from the same camera. | A different calibration cue and evaluation problem. This experiment uses sparse axial depths for supervision and the same registered camera geometry and endpoint for every arm. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24607) |

Differentiation through geometry optimization is established. BA-Net learns features and depth bases through bundle adjustment. Diff3R differentiates through scene refinement to learn an initialization and uncertainty regularization during training. The mathematical identity for the derivative of the five-scale ridge solve is therefore a method check. The incremental empirical question here concerns test-time adapter updates, forward-matched detachment, and a calibration compensation penalty against trace-matched damping. [BA-Net](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.04807) · [Diff3R](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01030)

These sources support a method comparison. The current screen provides no controlled numerical ranking against the official implementations above. CAPA is the closest comparator for any future sparse-depth accuracy claim. Test3R and LoRA3D can test whether image-only adaptation already accounts for an observed gain. If extending comparison across inputs, keep the same frames and output alignment, distinguish stages that receive measurements, and include adaptation and reconstruction time for each method.

The three 7-Scenes rooms are development material. Earlier and later measurement packets create two paired adaptation directions on the same rooms. Support and query influence adaptation, while the opposite packet supplies the fixed held-source development metric after a global prediction seal. The tracks are benchmark estimates and the depth projection uses the project’s documented preprocessing. Any dense, cross-dataset or deployment claim requires its own evidence. The final public evidence package can provide aggregate metrics, checks and replay code without publishing raw RGB/depth rasters, checkpoints or the new sampled measurement vectors. Full byte-level replay remains available from local archives and independently obtained dataset assets.
