# Registered test of per-view depth scales

We use the twelve existing 7-Scenes development conditions, nine distinct five-view RGB batches in Heads, Chess and Fire, with the existing sealed local RTX 3080 camera and depth heads. Every method gets the same images. The two metadata faults per scene share one base RGB batch. Direction and fault labels enter only in reporting. All computation for this test is local.

## Estimate geometry

Keep the frozen world-to-camera rotation, translation, principal point and depth shape. Use the supplied focal values on both axes, replacing the single metadata outlier with the other four views' median only when the previously published relative camera-head rule selects it. Use the relative rule without epipolar gating. The fixed-camera ray and camera center place a pixel at `C_i + s_i b_ip`, where `b_ip = R_i^T K_i^-1 [u,v,1]^T d_ip`. Read axial depth by bilinear interpolation at the pixel center. Discard matches outside the image or with a nonfinite or nonpositive depth in the interpolation footprint. Use all original matches in each connected-feature-disjoint fold.

Scale world distances by the median of the positive pairwise distances between frozen camera centers. Require a finite baseline, at least six valid correspondences for each retained pair, a connected graph covering all five views, and a full-column-rank stacked design on the fitting fold. Singularity is measured with singular value ratio <= 1e-6. A failed support or rank check returns identity scales. Fit fold zero. Minimize the equal-pair mean of vector Huber losses on the 3D disagreement, with transition 0.05 baseline units and an additional penalty 0.005 times the mean squared distance of the multipliers from one. Solve by bounded iteratively reweighted least squares for up to eight updates starting at one, stopping at a maximum scale change below 1e-7. Bounds are [1/1.15, 1.15]. Fold one uses the same pair weighting, support test and robust loss without regularization. Accept the five-scale proposal when held-back loss falls by at least 1% against identity, with starting loss above 1e-12. Rejection preserves the direct focal geometry. No reference geometry, test poses or valid-depth evaluation masks enter inference.

## Comparisons and decision

Report the local frozen FP32 predictions, direct focal replacement at identity depth, a single shared multiplier, five fitted multipliers before rejection, and the guarded five-scale result. The shared arm uses fold zero, with no second-fold rejection. Include historical RTX 3090 frozen native and 40-update retain, consensus and adaptive arms. Historical trained arms use the mean of both seeds per condition. Report all twelve conditions, fold support and worst scene. Reuse dense scores only for identical prediction tensors on the same reference and mask.

Use the existing Umeyama, rigid ICP and nearest-neighbor evaluator on both registered-RGB and released depth. For each reference and condition divide each method's accuracy and completeness distances by the corresponding local frozen FP32 distance. Average the two ratios and give the twelve conditions equal weight. Report normal consistency separately. The primary comparator is the strongest aggregate registered score among local frozen, direct focal and shared scale. Advancement requires at least 5% lower registered error than that comparator **and** the strongest of the four historical arms, neither registered distance component more than 2% worse than the local comparator, no scene worse than the local comparator on its combined score, and a released-depth score no higher than that comparator. Unguarded five-scale measures the contribution of the acceptance rule. In case of ties among the three local controls, use the listed order.

Report measured matching, frozen inference and correction times separately. Summed component measurements made on the same saved RGB request are an estimate of warm processing time. They cannot establish an end-to-end speed advantage over a different GPU or cold-start pipeline. The 5% decision is for development spending. The three previously examined scenes cannot demonstrate generalization.
